I · Session 1 · The doctrine

You never sell the aftermath. You sell the truth.

This is the most consequential thing said across both days. It is a commercial doctrine, not a moral one, and it governs the website, the club, the giving back, the second line and every sentence in Section III.

The impact is the end result, is the aftermath. You never sell the aftermath. You sell the truth. The truth, something nobody can deny, is whether you like your perfume or you don’t. Nizzar Ben Chekroune · S1 01:01:26

The chain he built from it, in one breath. If I feel good with my perfume, I feel good with myself. The moment I feel good with myself, I am being respected. I might not be liked or loved, but I am being respected, because people can see me standing still.

And the reason it is commercial rather than pious:

If we sell the impact, we become sellers. People think we are selling something to them. When we talk about the truth of what it is, the impact is going to come anyway, and they will always align with what we say. Nizzar Ben Chekroune · S1 01:03:01

What it costs. Everything that is easy to say. The house may not write that its perfume attracts love, opens a frequency or changes a room. All of those may be true for a given client. None may be sold. Françoise had already refused them on her own grounds: I don’t want to make fake promises, because there are so many parameters. S1 01:07:19

The enemy, named

Nizzar rejected the obvious diagnosis and produced a better one. Not the fear of missing out, which everybody already sells against.

The syndrome of being left behind.

The world is moving and I am left behind. Technology is advancing and I am left behind. It is deeper, it is even more vicious, because it is not a fear. It is a syndrome.

Françoise: it means it is systemic. And when it is used commercially, if you don’t buy that, you are not going to belong to that system or class, so you feel left behind.

The two problems, solved at once

Nizzar set the brief at S1 01:06:09: the presentation of the house has to solve the left-behind syndrome and answer who you are when you wear it, in the same move.

It does, and the mechanism is the doctrine above. A house that sells only the truth cannot make anyone feel behind, because there is no race in the proposition. There is a person, a reading and a bottle.

Anna arrived at the operative distinction herself: the story is not we give back, which nobody believes. The story is co-created, which is a fact about how the thing was made rather than a claim about the maker.

And the footnote, which belongs in a strategy document and nowhere else. Nizzar: Patagonia made sure that you know about it. That is the trick. The house briefs, and lets others write it.

This page resolves the tension Nizzar named on the first day and left open. Anna wanted forever, and she also wanted the next one. Read carelessly, the promise destroys the best revenue this house has. Read precisely, it creates it.

The rule, stated exactly

A formula is composed once, from one person, and is never composed for a second person. It is remade for its owner on request, for as long as the house exists.

Composing is the singular act. Remaking is service. Confusing the two means either selling the same reading twice, which ends the promise, or refusing a client her own perfume, which is absurd.

A person is allowed more than one portrait in a life. A second reading, years later, gets a second number, and the first stays in the register under its own name rather than being overwritten.
