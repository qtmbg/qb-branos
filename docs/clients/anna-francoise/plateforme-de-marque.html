A brand is not decided. It is collapsed out of everything the founders already carry, by putting them under enough pressure that only the load-bearing answers survive.

Why the exercises were shaped the way they were

Three exercises ran on 18 September, and their design was deliberate. The first was open, and gave the feeling of control. The second was playful. The third dictated the terms completely. That escalation is the method: it moves the founders from stating opinions to defending them.

The first exercise was more free. You had the feeling that you were in control. That wasn’t the case, but I gave you the feeling that you were in control. Nizzar Ben Chekroune · 00:06:32

Reason for being, mission, vision.

Three statements that do three different jobs. The first explains why the house should exist at all. The second is what it does on a Tuesday. The third is the state of the world it is working toward, and it has to be large enough to survive being reached.

Reason for being

Because a person is already someone, and almost nothing in the world says so back.

The category sells becoming. Wear this and you will be desired, powerful, free. The premise is that you are not yet. This house was founded by two people who do not believe that, and one of them refused the word for it out loud.

Mission

To read one person closely enough that the formula could not fit anyone else, and to go and find whatever it takes to build it.

Two verbs, and they belong to the two founders. Reading is the consultation. Finding is the sourcing, including materials the nose has never used, ordered for one commission.

Vision

A house whose clients recognise each other before they are introduced.

It is the club, stated as an outcome instead of as a feature. It also contains the commercial ambition honestly: recognition at that level is what makes referral the only channel the house needs.

Anna’s five-year frame, and Françoise’s circle of influence, arriving at the same place.

How to use these three

They are not copy. None of the three sentences above should appear on a website as written. They are the test a piece of work has to pass before it ships.

When you are decidingAsk
Whether to take a commissionCan we read this person? If the client wants a scent they already imagined, the house is being used as a supplier. Refuse or reframe.
Whether to add a productDoes it come out of reading one person? A candle does not. An establishment accord does, because a hotel is a subject that can be read.
Whether a sentence is on-brandDoes it tell the reader they are lacking something? If yes, it is off-brand regardless of how well it is written.
Whether to hire someoneWill we be told what to do by this person within a year? If not, the house has hired a pair of hands.

What is missing is not the power. It is the proof.

It holds the Magician and the Lover at once, it survives translation into French, and it is the shortest expression of the position the three exercises converged on.

It is a candidate for the public line. It is not yet the tagline, because the name is not settled and the two are chosen together. Page 39.

Six beliefs, and what each one costs.

A belief that costs nothing is a slogan. Each of these removes an option the house could otherwise have taken, and the removed option is printed next to it. If a cost looks unacceptable, the belief is the thing to argue with, not the cost.

We believeBecauseWhat it costs us
Power is not given. Nobody walks in here incomplete. This came out of the room as a refusal, not as a position, which is why it is first. The entire persuasion engine of the category. The house cannot sell aspiration, and aspiration is what sells fragrance.
Quality outlives the price, but only if the price never moves. Both founders claimed Gucci’s line. Read as an instruction rather than a compliment, it is about pricing behaviour. Sales, gifting promotions, outlet, seasonal offers, friends-of-the-house rates. One discount and the line becomes decoration.
A material has an origin, a season and a person behind it. Françoise buys from producers she knows. That is a supply chain and it is also the only verifiable claim in a category full of unverifiable ones. Margin. Naming the origin means buying at the harvest from a producer who can be named, not at the best quote from a broker.
Magic is accuracy. The Magician was chosen twice, and both readings described perception, not conjuring. What reads as magic is someone having paid an unusual amount of attention. The promise on page 16 says a formula is made once. Read carelessly, that sentence destroys the best revenue a house like this has. Read precisely, it creates it. The distinction is the most commercially important paragraph in this document.

The rule, stated exactly

A formula is composed once, from one person, and is never composed for a second person. It is remade for its owner on request, for as long as the house exists.

  • The reading is not interrupted. No telephone in the room, no second appointment that day, no fixed end time. A sitting that ran over is a sitting that worked.
  • The house accord is off before a client arrives. Page 34. A nose cannot read a person through her own house.
  • Nothing is proposed in the first hour. Page 07. The client is listened to for longer than is comfortable, and the discomfort is the product.
  • One working day, always. Including when the answer is not ready. I do not know yet, I will know on Thursday is an answer.
