## 04 · The six phases

| Phase | Name | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| 00 | Acquisition | The free entry point. Signal Scan diagnostic. Lead capture into the system. |
| 01 | Discovery | The four diagnostic instruments. Brand Soul Map, Sensescape, Visual DNA, War Table. The QBP is built here. |
| 02 | Brand Creation | Logo Direction, Logo Evaluation, Voice Guide. The brand identity becomes operational. |
| 03 | Content Creation | Instagram Seed, LinkedIn Strategy, YouTube Strategy, Newsletter Architecture, Content Bridge. The brand becomes a publishing system. |
| 04 | Execution | Content Repurposing Engine, Content Scheduler. The publishing system runs. |
| 05 | Intelligence | Brand Performance Dashboard, Quarterly Brand Review, Predictive Panel. The brand learns and adapts. |

The six phases are sequential by design. The user is meant to walk from acquisition through intelligence as their brand matures. The system supports entry at any phase, but the canonical journey is the full sequence.

Phase 00 (acquisition) and Phase 01 (discovery) are free. Phases 02 through 05 are paid (Starter tier and above).

## 05 · The twenty agents

The complete agent inventory. Each agent reads from and writes to the QBP.

**Phase 00. Acquisition.**

Signal Scan. The free brand diagnostic. Five-minute audit producing a first insight and lead capture.

**Phase 01. Discovery.**

Brand Soul Map. Surfaces the core truth of the brand. Produces brand essence, the spark, archetype, manifesto, anti-brand, paradox, always-and-never.

Sensescape. Surfaces the sensory and emotional world of the brand. Produces sensory profile across the five senses, mood board direction, sonic identity direction.

Visual DNA. Surfaces the visual language of the brand. Produces colour direction, typography direction, visual reference inventory.

War Table. Pressure-tests the brand against competitive and strategic context. Produces competitor landscape, market position, strategic priorities, the brand's defensive and offensive moves.

**Phase 02. Brand Creation.**

Logo Direction. Generates logo concepts grounded in the QBP. Outputs three to five direction explorations.

Logo Evaluation. Evaluates existing logos against the QBP. Outputs verdict: keep, refine, or rebuild, with rationale.

Voice Guide. Generates the brand's full voice specification: tone anchors, sentence rhythm, banned phrases, surface-by-surface guidance, written examples.

**Phase 03. Content Creation.**

Instagram Seed. Generates a launch content set for Instagram (twelve posts, captions, hashtag strategy).

LinkedIn Strategy. Generates a LinkedIn presence strategy and twenty-post launch set.

YouTube Strategy. Generates a YouTube channel strategy and six initial video scripts.

Newsletter Architecture. Generates a newsletter strategy: cadence, sections, voice, first three issues.

Content Bridge. Generates the connective tissue between channels: how Instagram supports LinkedIn supports YouTube supports newsletter, all reading from the same QBP.

**Phase 04. Execution.**

Content Repurposing Engine. Takes one input (essay, talk, podcast, manifesto) and produces channel-adapted versions across Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, newsletter, and short-form video.

Content Scheduler. Schedules and distributes the produced content across platforms (via Buffer integration).

**Phase 05. Intelligence.**

Brand Performance Dashboard. Tracks the brand's performance across channels, audiences, and outputs. Surfaces signal versus noise.

Quarterly Brand Review. Generates a quarterly review of brand performance, learning, and direction. Produces the brief for the next quarter.

Predictive Panel. Simulates market response to a planned move (launch, campaign, repositioning) before the move is made. Pro tier. Five runs per month.

Twenty agents. Six phases. One QBP. One product.

## 07 · The Weakest Persona Principle

The system is designed for the weakest persona: the user who arrives with one idea and zero assets.

If the Blank Slate is fully served, every other persona is automatically covered. They enter at a later stage of the same system.

This principle is non-negotiable. Every design decision, copy decision, illustration decision, and tool decision is checked against the Blank Slate's experience first.

The Doubter, the Player, and the Multi-Brand are downstream beneficiaries. They are not the design constraint. The Blank Slate is the design constraint.

## 09 · The voice

The QB voice is documented in full in the QB Voice Codex.

QB sounds like a thoughtful founder, not a software company. Direct. Calm. Slightly contrarian. Aware of the weight of identity work. Trustful of the reader.

Em dashes are absent. Exclamation points are absent outside literal in-product celebration moments. Banned phrases (empower, unlock, supercharge, seamless, AI-powered, "take to the next level", "stand out from the crowd", in order to, at the end of the day, world-class, best-in-class, cutting-edge, robust, frictionless, journey as user-path) are absent.

The voice is the same across marketing pages, in-product copy, AI agent responses, error messages, empty states, emails, social posts, and white-label client output. Voice anchors are locked. Mechanics flex slightly by surface.

## 10 · The visual language

The QB visual language is documented in full in the QB Design System v3.5.

Cream surfaces (`#FBF5E6`) and aubergine ink (`#2D1521`) are the foundation. There is no neutral grey middle ground.

The brand triad is gold (`#E0B069`) for primary action, rose (`#CA6180`) for the brand mark motif, teal (`#9ED3DC`) for the system pulse.

Six phase colours, one per phase, used in tags and dots only.

Three typefaces: Fraunces (serif), Inter Tight (body), JetBrains Mono (system).

Six visual signatures: cream-and-ink, the two-layer 3D pill button, the hard offset shadow, the eyebrow tag plus serif headline rhythm, fluid clamp type and space, editorial illustrations inside cream-card frames.

The brand mark is a Q-and-B infinity figure with a clarifying tail, locked SVG paths, four colourways (ink, gold, rose, reverse cream), two locked lockups (horizontal, vertical).

The wordmark is "quantum branding" in Fraunces italic 600, SOFT axis 60, lowercase. Always lowercase.

## 13 · The white-label model

The Agency tier supports up to eight client portals, each with its own white-label configuration.

Each client portal carries the agency's branding. The QB methodology runs underneath. The agency's logo, colours, and voice surface to the client. The QB system is invisible from the client's perspective unless the agency chooses to disclose it.

White-label client output (content drafts, brand profiles, strategic deliverables) is generated by the QB agents reading from the client's QBP. The voice flexes to match the client. The QB voice anchors hold the chassis. The client's voice supplies the paint.

The white-label is not a thin theme layer. It is full multi-tenant infrastructure: separate QBPs per client, separate user accounts per client team, separate billing where appropriate, separate analytics dashboards.

## 16 · Anti-patterns

These are the patterns that are absent from the QB system. They are absent because they violate the brand truth, the visual truth, the writing truth, or the methodology.

Invented testimonials. Invented credentials. Invented metrics. Invented case studies. The system uses real engagements only.

Manufactured urgency. Countdown timers. "Last chance" framing. "Only X spots left" framing. The system trusts the reader's ability to decide.

Folksy headlines. Stand-up-comedy openings. "Hey there!" "Pro tip" "Real talk" patterns. The voice is editorial, not casual.

SaaS-cliché vocabulary. The banned phrase list in the Voice Codex is enforced.

AI-tells. Em dashes used as rhythm devices. Bullet point lists where prose would do. "Not just X but Y" patterns. Formulaic comparison structures. The voice is human first.

Visual anti-patterns. Soft drop shadows with blur. Glassmorphism. Neumorphism. Gradient mesh backgrounds. Animated gradients. Particle effects. Custom cursors. Marquees. Tickers. Parallax. These are absent from the QB visual system.

Illustration anti-patterns. Stock-illustration tropes (giant smartphones with text bubbles, crowds of identical figures pointing at charts, lightbulb moments above heads). Hyper-detail. Photorealism. Painterly textures. Six-fingered hands. Garbled signage. These are absent from the QB illustration system.

Quantum jargon. "Quantum" outside the brand name itself. "Quantum leap." "Quantum thinking." "Quantum-powered." Absent.

The presence of any anti-pattern is an error to be corrected, not a stylistic choice.
