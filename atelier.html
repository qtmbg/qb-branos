What this is

Atelier is one engagement at a time, run with me personally. The QB BrandOS methodology, at a different resolution than self-serve.

It exists outside the tier structure because some brand work does not survive being templated. Scope and shape follow what the work requires, not the other way around.

No two engagements are the same. The page describes the door. The work is defined inside.

How it works

You write. Tell me what you're working on and what you would want this to be.

I read. If I think we should talk, I write back, and we have a conversation. If I don't, I'll tell you that too, and recommend whichever self-serve tier fits.

If we move forward, scope, format, and investment get shaped together. The terms come from the work.
